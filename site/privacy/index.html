Privacy notice

This notice covers every service the Gunmetal project runs itself. Today that is one: this website, gunmetal.tv, including its invitation landing page and any file published here, such as the update and advisory feed once it exists. It does not cover a Gunmetal server that someone runs for themselves. Each server shows its own privacy notice, generated from its own settings, before anyone accepts an invitation to it.

What this site is

gunmetal.tv serves only fixed files: pages, text files and response headers. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms, no comments and no database. It runs no script in your browser, sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

No analytics, trackers or third parties

This site loads nothing from any other site: no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no fonts or scripts from a content network and no embedded videos or social media buttons. Its pages tell your browser to refuse anything from another origin. Links to other sites, such as the source code on GitHub, load nothing until you follow them.

What is recorded when you visit

Like any website, the hosting service that delivers these files sees each request: your IP address, the time, the address of the page you asked for and what your browser says about itself. These request logs are kept for no more than 30 days, are used only to keep the site running and to stop abuse, and are not used to profile anyone.

When you open an invitation link, the secret after the # sign is never sent to this site, so it never appears in these logs.

Your rights

Because the site keeps nothing about you beyond those short-lived request logs, there is normally nothing to show you, correct or delete. If you have a question about your data or want to use a right you have under data protection law, open an issue in the project's issue tracker, or report privately as the security policy describes.

Changes

The text of this notice is kept in the project's public source code, so every change to it is recorded there.

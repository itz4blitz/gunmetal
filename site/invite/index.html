You have been invited to a Gunmetal server

Someone who runs a Gunmetal media server has invited you to use it. Gunmetal is free, open-source software that people run at home for themselves, their family and their friends.

Your invitation stays private

The secret that makes your invitation work is the part of the link after the # sign. Your browser never sends that part to gunmetal.tv, so it never reaches this site or its logs. This page runs no script and loads nothing from any other site, so nothing here can read the secret or pass it on.

What to do next

  1. Install the Gunmetal app on the device you want to listen on.
  2. Open the whole invitation link, exactly as you received it, in the app. The app reads the secret, connects to the server that invited you and shows you that server's privacy notice before you accept.
  3. Choose how you sign in. Your account lives on that server, not on gunmetal.tv.

An invitation works once and expires after a few days. If yours has expired, ask the person who sent it for a new one.

Gunmetal is pre-alpha software and has no released app yet.
